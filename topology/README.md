# Operational Topology

How the moving parts connect: governed workflows, visibility, accountability, and feedback loops across operational work.

Back to portfolio index: [README.md](../README.md)

## Overview

Without a topology, people stay busy but the organization can't see what's happening, who owns it, or what changed. Work starts as a need, request, obligation, or gap, and becomes a governed workflow with an owner, documentation, and evidence. As it crosses domains, accountability shifts, but it doesn't disappear into inboxes or memory.

This folder holds the system-level diagrams behind the smaller demos.
