**Internal Discovery Questions for the Program Team**

**Scenario Assumption:** The program is considering a new SaaS software platform with 100 initial users, potential growth to 300 users over three years, and annual subscription licensing.

**1. Mission Need and Business Requirements**

- What mission or business problem is the software intended to solve?
- What capabilities are required on Day 1 versus future phases?
- Is this replacing an existing tool or introducing a new capability?
- What would happen if implementation were delayed by 3–6 months?
- Are there any mandatory technical, security, or compliance requirements?

**2. User Population and License Needs**

- Who are the initial 100 users?
- Do all users require the same license level or feature set?
- Are there users who could utilize lower-cost license tiers?
- How frequently will users access the system?
- Are contractor personnel included in the user count?
- Will licenses need to be reassigned as personnel change?

**3. Growth Assumptions**

- What drives the projected increase from 100 to 300 users?
- Is growth expected gradually or in specific phases?
- How confident is the growth estimate?
- What is the expected user count at the end of each year?
- Is future growth already funded or only anticipated?
- If growth does not occur, what is the minimum user population likely to remain?

**4. Budget and Funding**

- What funding is available for the initial subscription period?
- What funding is anticipated for future years?
- Is there a target annual budget ceiling?
- Is the program more sensitive to upfront cost or long-term cost?
- Are there budget constraints that would limit future expansion?
- Would the program accept a longer commitment in exchange for pricing protections?

**5. Deployment and Support Requirements**

- Is implementation assistance required?
- Will vendor-led onboarding or training be necessary?
- Are dedicated support resources required?
- Are there uptime, availability, or service-level expectations?
- Will administrators require advanced training or certification?

**6. Technical and Integration Requirements**

- Does the software need to integrate with existing systems?
- Are there data migration requirements?
- Are there identity management or single sign-on requirements?
- Are there security authorization considerations that could affect implementation?
- Are there future integrations that should be considered during negotiations?

**7. Contract Flexibility Requirements**

- Will user counts likely fluctuate during the contract period?
- Is the ability to add licenses mid-year important?
- Is the ability to reduce licenses at renewal important?
- Does the program need flexibility to change license tiers over time?
- Are there concerns about vendor lock-in?

**8. Acquisition Strategy Considerations**

- Are alternative products acceptable if pricing becomes unfavorable?
- Has market research identified comparable solutions?
- Is direct purchase from the vendor preferred, or are reseller options acceptable?
- Are there existing contract vehicles or acquisition pathways that should be considered?
- Are there schedule pressures that may affect negotiating leverage?

**9. Renewal and Long-Term Planning**

- How long is the software expected to remain part of the program’s environment?
- What would a successful three-year outcome look like?
- Is continuity of service critical at renewal?
- What concerns exist regarding future price increases?
- How difficult would it be to transition to another solution if necessary?

---

**Potential Negotiation Levers Identified**

Based on the current scenario, Procurement may have several potential sources of leverage to validate before engaging the vendor:

- Initial commitment of 100 users.
- Potential growth to 300 users over the program lifecycle.
- Multi-year relationship potential despite annual subscriptions.
- Opportunity for future license expansion.
- Ability to evaluate competing products or acquisition channels.
- Potential willingness to trade contract duration, payment timing, or deployment schedule for improved pricing or additional value.

**Procurement Objective Before Vendor Engagement:** Validate growth assumptions, funding availability, license requirements, and flexibility needs so the government enters negotiations with clearly defined requirements and a documented understanding of where leverage exists.
