# **SaaS Negotiation Strategy**

**Scenario:**  
The government is evaluating **CodeForge Ultimate**, a fictional cloud-based DevSecOps platform.

**Current Requirement**

- 100 users
- Annual subscription licensing

**Projected Growth**

- Up to 300 users over three years

**Vendor Information Gathered (Illustrative Demo Data Only)**

|**Item**|**OEM (CodeForge Technologies)**|
|---|---|
|Public List Price|$1,500/user/year|
|List Price @ 100 Users|$150,000|
|Initial OEM Offer|$135,000 (10% discount)|
|Multi-Year Discount|Available with pricing commitment|
|Expansion Pricing|Not yet defined|
|Renewal Increase Cap|Not offered initially|
|Training|Optional add-on|
|Implementation Support|Available at additional cost|

---

# **Negotiation Objectives**

## **Primary Objective 1: Reduce Initial Cost**

The vendor’s initial offer of $135,000 reflects a discount from published pricing, but additional opportunities may exist.

**Target Outcome**

- Additional discount based on expected growth
- Improved value through bundled services
- Better pricing than standard commercial rates

---

## **Primary Objective 2: Lock Future Growth Pricing**

The projected increase from 100 to 300 users represents significant future revenue for the vendor.

**Goal**  
Negotiate pricing protections now rather than accepting future market pricing.

**Desired Outcome**

|**User Range**|**Target Discount**|
|---|---|
|100 Users|10%+|
|101–200 Users|15%|
|201–300 Users|20%|

This creates predictable costs as the program expands.

---

## **Primary Objective 3: Protect Against Renewal Price Shock**

Renewal costs often exceed initial-year negotiations.

**Desired Outcome**

- Renewal increase cap
- Multi-year pricing schedule
- Defined methodology for future pricing

---

## **Primary Objective 4: Maximize Total Value**

Focus not only on license pricing but also on included services.

Potential additions:

- Administrator training
- End-user onboarding
- Technical workshops
- Implementation assistance
- Dedicated customer success support

---

# **Pricing Opportunities**

## **Opportunity 1: Growth Commitment**

The vendor benefits from potential growth from 100 to 300 users.

Negotiation position:

“We cannot commit to future quantities today, but projected growth should be reflected in current pricing and future expansion rates.”

---

## **Opportunity 2: Multi-Year Relationship**

Although subscriptions are funded annually, the anticipated program duration is three years.

Potential request:

- Multi-year pricing schedule
- Locked discount structure
- Stable expansion pricing

---

## **Opportunity 3: Bundled Services**

Training and implementation support may have greater flexibility than license pricing.

If additional discounts are limited:

Request inclusion of:

- Administrator training
- Deployment assistance
- Adoption workshops

at no additional cost.

---

# **Volume Discount Opportunities**

Request a predefined volume pricing schedule.

### **Proposed Structure**

|**Users**|**Target Price/User**|
|---|---|
|100|$1,350|
|200|$1,275|
|300|$1,200|

### **Benefits**

- Predictable budgeting
- Simplified future ordering
- Reduced negotiation effort during expansion

---

# **Future Expansion Considerations**

## **Additional User Pricing**

Request that future licenses receive the same or better discount level.

Questions to resolve:

- Will future licenses be priced at current rates?
- Will additional users receive volume-based discounts?
- Are expansions co-termed with existing subscriptions?

---

## **Scalability**

Request confirmation that:

- Additional licenses can be added throughout the year
- Expansion does not trigger repricing of existing licenses
- New users can be added without minimum purchase requirements

---

# **Contract Terms**

## **Licensing Flexibility**

Request:

- License reassignment rights
- Ability to add users during the subscription period
- Co-terminous licensing for administrative simplicity

---

## **Support Requirements**

Request inclusion of:

- Standard support
- Service-level commitments
- Escalation procedures

---

## **Data Portability**

Request clear language covering:

- Data export
- Offboarding support
- Access to customer data at contract expiration

---

# **Renewal Protections**

## **Preferred Position**

- Renewal increase cap of 3% annually

## **Acceptable Position**

- Renewal increase cap of 5% annually

## **Additional Protection**

Request:

- Written renewal methodology
- Advance notice of pricing changes
- Preservation of negotiated discount levels

---

# **Risks**

## **Risk: Future Growth Pricing Undefined**

**Impact**  
Future expansion may occur at significantly higher rates.

**Mitigation**  
Negotiate expansion pricing schedule before award.

---

## **Risk: Renewal Cost Increases**

**Impact**  
Budget instability and reduced negotiating leverage.

**Mitigation**  
Establish renewal protections during initial negotiations.

---

## **Risk: Vendor Lock-In**

**Impact**  
Transitioning to another platform becomes costly.

**Mitigation**  
Ensure data export and transition support provisions are defined.

---

## **Risk: Overcommitting to Growth**

**Impact**  
Paying for licenses that are never utilized.

**Mitigation**  
Avoid contractual minimums beyond current requirements.

---

# **Fallback Positions**

## **Preferred Outcome**

- Initial discount greater than 10%
- Volume pricing through 300 users
- Included training
- 3% renewal cap
- Expansion pricing locked

---

## **Acceptable Outcome**

- Initial discount maintained
- Defined pricing tiers for future growth
- Reduced-cost training package
- 5% renewal cap

---

## **Minimum Acceptable Position**

- Expansion pricing documented
- Annual renewal protections established
- No mandatory user growth commitments
- Clear support and data portability terms

---

# **Procurement Position Summary**

The strongest leverage available is the vendor’s opportunity to grow from **100 users to 300 users over a three-year period**. Procurement should focus negotiations on converting that future growth potential into immediate value through **volume discounts, expansion pricing protections, renewal caps, and bundled services**, while avoiding commitments that require the government to purchase licenses beyond actual requirements.
