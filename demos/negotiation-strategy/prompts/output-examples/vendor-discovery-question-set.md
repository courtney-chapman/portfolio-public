# **Vendor Discovery Questions**

**Scenario Assumption:** The government anticipates an initial requirement of 100 users, potential growth to 300 users over a three-year period, and annual SaaS subscription licensing.

The purpose of these questions is to understand pricing structure, future scalability, contract flexibility, and long-term cost implications before entering negotiations.

---

# **1. Volume Pricing**

### **Current Requirement (100 Users)**

- What is your standard pricing model for 100 users?
- Are volume discounts available at the current user count?
- At what user thresholds do additional discounts become available?
- Are discounts based on total users, annual spend, or contract value?
- Are there any promotional or government-specific pricing programs available?

### **Future Growth**

- How would pricing change if the user count increased to 200 users?
- How would pricing change if the user count increased to 300 users?
- Can future user growth be included in today’s pricing discussions?

---

# **2. Growth Discounts and Expansion Pricing**

- Can expansion pricing be established in advance for future user growth?
- If additional licenses are added during the contract term, will they receive the same discounted rate as the original purchase?
- Are expansion purchases subject to current market pricing or pre-negotiated pricing?
- Is there a formal true-up process?
- How frequently are true-ups conducted?
- Are there minimum purchase quantities for future expansions?

### **Example Clarification**

- If the government purchases 100 licenses today and expands to 250 users next year, how would pricing be calculated?

---

# **3. Multi-Year Commitments**

- Are additional discounts available for customers expecting a multi-year relationship?
- Can pricing be locked for multiple years while maintaining annual ordering?
- Are annual price increases capped?
- Is there a discount structure associated with projected long-term growth?
- Can future expansion pricing be guaranteed during a multi-year period?

### **Pricing Stability**

- Under what circumstances can pricing change during the relationship?
- How are future subscription rates determined?

---

# **4. License Tiers and User Segmentation**

- What license tiers are available?
- What functionality differentiates each tier?
- Do all users require the same license type?
- Can different user populations use different license tiers?
- Are lower-cost options available for occasional users?
- Are read-only or limited-access licenses available?

### **License Administration**

- Can licenses be reassigned between users?
- Are there limits on license transfers?
- How are inactive users handled?

---

# **5. Training and Enablement**

- What training resources are included with the subscription?
- Is administrator training included?
- Is end-user training included?
- Are self-paced training materials available?
- Are virtual instructor-led courses available?

### **Negotiation Opportunities**

- Can training be included at no additional cost?
- Can onboarding workshops be bundled into the subscription?
- Are additional training credits available for expanding organizations?

---

# **6. Implementation and Support**

- What onboarding services are included?
- What implementation assistance is available?
- Is a dedicated customer success manager assigned?
- What support levels are available?

### **Service Levels**

- What are the standard support response times?
- What service-level commitments are included?
- Are premium support options available?

### **Growth Support**

- How does the vendor support organizations that significantly increase user counts during the contract period?

---

# **7. Renewal Protections**

- How is renewal pricing determined?
- Are renewal increases capped?
- Can renewal pricing protections be negotiated at initial award?
- Are customers protected from significant pricing changes after the initial term?

### **Long-Term Cost Predictability**

- What historical factors typically drive renewal price increases?
- Can pricing protections extend through multiple renewal periods?

---

# **8. Future Expansion Flexibility**

- How quickly can additional licenses be added?
- Is there a minimum quantity for expansion purchases?
- Are expanded licenses co-termed with existing subscriptions?
- Can license quantities increase at any time during the year?

### **Growth Scenario**

- If user counts increase from 100 to 300 over three years, what commercial programs exist to support that growth?

---

# **9. Contract Flexibility and Exit Planning**

- What options exist if actual growth is lower than projected?
- Can license quantities be adjusted at renewal?
- What happens if the government does not renew?
- What data export capabilities are available at contract expiration?
- What offboarding support is provided?

---

# **Key Information Procurement Should Obtain**

Before moving into negotiations, Procurement should seek clear answers to the following:

|**Area**|**Desired Outcome**|
|---|---|
|Volume Pricing|Defined discount thresholds|
|Growth Pricing|Pre-negotiated expansion rates|
|Multi-Year Relationship|Pricing stability and future protections|
|License Structure|Appropriate tiering for different users|
|Training|Included onboarding and user enablement|
|Support|Clearly defined service levels|
|Renewals|Protection against excessive price increases|
|Expansion|Flexible scaling without pricing penalties|

**Procurement Objective:** Establish a complete understanding of how pricing, licensing, growth, support, and renewals will be handled so future expansion can be planned and negotiated before the initial award.
