# **Requirement Assessment**

## **Known Information**

|**Item**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|Quantity|~75 ruggedized laptops|
|Budget|~$180,000|
|Delivery|Within 60 days|
|Environment|Federal contractor operations|
|Users|Frequent travelers / field personnel|
|Accessories|Multiple peripherals required|

---

## **Missing Requirements**

### **Technical**

- Minimum processor, memory, and storage requirements
- Operating system preference
- Display size and battery-life expectations
- Ruggedization standard required (e.g., field use vs. extreme environments)

### **Security**

- Encryption and authentication requirements
- Federal or agency-specific security controls
- Peripheral connectivity restrictions

### **Logistics**

- Delivery location(s)
- Deployment schedule
- Warranty and support requirements

### **Accessories**

- Docking stations
- External monitors
- Vehicle mounts
- Spare batteries and chargers

---

## **Acquisition Risks**

- Budget may not align with desired ruggedized configurations and accessories.
- Incomplete technical requirements may limit meaningful vendor comparisons.
- Ruggedized equipment can have longer manufacturing and delivery lead times.
- Security and compliance requirements may significantly affect product selection.
- Peripheral compatibility issues may create additional deployment costs.

---

## **Information Needed Before Market Research**

1. Desired technical specifications and performance requirements.
2. Required ruggedization level and operating environment.
3. Security and compliance requirements.
4. Complete list of peripherals and accessories.
5. Delivery locations and deployment schedule.
6. Warranty, maintenance, and lifecycle support expectations.

### **Recommended Next Step**

Finalize minimum technical, security, and accessory requirements before evaluating vendors or pricing. This will improve market research accuracy, support price comparisons, and reduce acquisition risk.
